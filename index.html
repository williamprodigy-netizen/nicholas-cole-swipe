What matters

Three funnels, three commitment levels, one audience

A free quiz, a five-day live sprint and a paid trial all run at the same buyer. Nobody is asked to jump further than they are ready to. We run one front door.

The sprint sells decisions, not lessons

Five days, one decision per day — name it, pick the platform, write, grow, monetise — ending with the thing actually launched. The deadline is structural rather than a discount expiring, and the outcome is a completed asset.

The quiz output is the lead magnet

The promise is not a PDF but a personalised plan: your niche, your offer, your acquisition system. Personalisation is the value, which also makes the answers usable as qualification data.

They name the failure state precisely

Their copy describes opening the platform, staring at the naming screen, closing the tab and promising to do it next weekend. Naming the exact procrastination loop does more than any benefit claim.
